# Glowburrow course assets

A curated sample of art and review captures from **Glowburrow**, a cozy village-building game made with AI tools.
It is shared to illustrate a course on making games with AI: what the assets look like, and how each one was checked
against written quality standards before it was accepted.

This is a selection of roughly a third of the game's models, clips and review captures. The game's source code,
specifications, generation scripts and prompts are not included. Use is limited by [TERMS.md](TERMS.md).

## What is inside

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `3d-models/buildings` | Seven buildings, each in three upgrade tiers (FBX). `tiers_<Building>.png` shows the tiers side by side. |
| `3d-models/characters` | Bloop the tutorial guide, the shadow visitor cast (eight species plus accessories) and three animal visitors, rigged and animated. |
| `3d-models/decor`, `environment`, `garden`, `props` | Decorations, landmarks, a vegetable garden in three tiers and small held props. |
| `animation/bloop` | Bloop's motion reviews and showreel, rendered from Blender. |
| `animation/shadow-cast` | The whole shadow cast performing nine motions, rendered from Blender. |
| `clips/gameplay` | Short portrait clips recorded in the running game: placing a building, repainting, growing a garden, upgrading, weather and wildlife. |
| `clips/wildlife` | Bird flight and landing motion reviews. |
| `blender-diorama` | Five self-contained Blender scenes built from the game's assets, with their rendered teaser clips in `renders/`. |
| `art-2d` | AI-generated visitor emote icons and illustrated visitor portraits. |
| `process` | Before and after comparisons and an all-sides review, showing how assets were revised to meet the standards. |

Each model folder holds the FBX file and, where available, a `qa/` folder with its review captures.

## The workflow these files illustrate

1. **Generate or author.** Animal visitors were generated by an external AI 3D service from front, side and back
   reference images in a single frozen T-pose. Buildings, props and the shadow cast were authored by AI coding agents
   as parameterized Blender scripts. Emote icons were generated as 2D images.
2. **Hold every asset to written standards.** The art direction sets soft, chunky forms, rounded exposed edges, a matte
   finish, a small fixed colour palette and a clear hierarchy of large masses. A building must keep its identity from
   all four camera rotations and at its lower level of detail.
3. **Review with captures, not opinions.** Each asset is captured the same way so problems are easy to spot:
   - `preview_neutral` and `review_iso`: neutral lighting at gameplay distance;
   - `review_rot1`–`review_rot3` and `preview_back`: the other three sides;
   - `review_silhouette_iso`: whether the shape reads from its outline alone;
   - `review_flat_iso`: unlit colour, to catch colour bleeding and stray islands;
   - `preview_lod1` and `review_lod1`: the lower level of detail;
   - `unity_morning`: the asset inside the game engine.
4. **Revise until it passes.** A technical pass is never treated as visual approval; a person signs off. The
   `process/` sheets show assets before and after a rebuild.

## Formats

- Models: FBX, 1 unit = 1 metre, Y up, pivot on the ground. Colour is stored as vertex colour with one opaque and one
  emissive material, so there are no texture files.
- Clips: H.264 MP4 without audio; GIFs for short loops.
- Blender scenes: `.blend` files saved with Blender 5.2; open them in Blender 5.x.
